# 🪞 Weekly review

**What it does**: once a week, it reads your helpers' own notebooks — every log and note they wrote this week — and hands you the recap: what they did for you ("sorted 63 emails, archived 21, flagged 9 for reply, warned you about 2 renewals"), what patterns they noticed, and the short list of things that still need a decision from **you**.

**What it never does**: touch your email, your calendar (beyond its one recap event), or anything outside the kit's own notebook. It's the only routine whose subject is the other routines.

**Needs**: Google Calendar (optional, for the recap event). Nothing else — its raw material is your own repository.

**Runs**: weekly, at a moment you'd actually read it (Friday afternoon or Sunday evening).

**Where you see it**: a calendar event — "🪞 your week: helpers handled 84 things, 3 need you" — plus a fuller recap saved in the notebook, so the story of any week is always there to reread.

**Good first routine?** Add it once two or three others are running — it's the one that makes the whole system feel accountable.
