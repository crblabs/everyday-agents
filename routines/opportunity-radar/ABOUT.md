# 📡 Opportunity radar

**What it does**: once a month, it looks for the *next* helper you should have — by reading what your existing helpers logged, and (if Gmail is connected) the month's fresh patterns in your email and calendar that no current helper covers. When it finds something real, it proposes **exactly one** idea, with its evidence attached: "you manually forwarded 11 receipts to yourself this month — a paper-trail helper would have caught all of them; want it?"

**What it never does**: propose more than one thing (a list of ideas is noise; one evidenced idea is a gift), suggest something generic it didn't actually observe, install or schedule anything itself — it points, you decide — or ping you on a month with nothing worth saying. Read-only everywhere.

**Needs**: nothing beyond what you've already connected — it works from your helpers' own notebooks, and sees more when Gmail/Calendar are connected. Google Calendar (optional) gives its rare pings a place to land.

**Runs**: monthly.

**Where you see it**: a calendar event only on months it found something — "📡 one idea for you" — most months, silence.

**Good first routine?** No — it's the *last* one you add, once two or three helpers are running and writing notebooks it can learn from. It's how the kit keeps discovering your life after the first conversation is long over.
