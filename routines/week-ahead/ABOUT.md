# 🗓 Week ahead

**What it does**: once a week (Sunday evening works well), it looks at your coming week and hands you the shape of it — the commitments, the crunch days, the conflicts or double-bookings, and the two or three things worth preparing before Monday makes them urgent.

**What it never does**: change your calendar in any way beyond leaving its one summary event. No accepting, declining, moving, or emailing.

**Needs**: Google Calendar connected; Gmail optional (it enriches "worth preparing" with open threads).

**Runs**: weekly, on the day and time you choose.

**Where you see it**: a calendar event — "🗓 your week: 9 meetings, Thursday is heavy" — with the full lookahead in its description.

**Good first routine?** Great second routine, once the morning brief has proven itself. Together they cover the day and the week.
