# ☕ Morning brief

**What it does**: before you're up, it assembles your day onto one phone screen — today's meetings with a prep note for each ("you haven't answered Ana's agenda question"), anything urgent-looking in your inbox, and the one thing that most deserves your first hour. It arrives as a small event at the top of your calendar day.

**What it never does**: accept or decline anything, move meetings, send mail, or reply to anyone. It briefs; you decide.

**Needs**: Google Calendar connected; Gmail too for the "anything urgent" section (optional — it works calendar-only).

**Runs**: every weekday, early morning (you pick the time; before your alarm is the trick).

**Where you see it**: a calendar event titled with the day's verdict — "☕ light day, 2 meetings" or "☕ full day — see prep notes" — with the full brief in its description.

**Good first routine?** Yes, if your days are meeting-shaped. The morning it first appears is the moment this kit clicks for most people.
