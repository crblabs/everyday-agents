# 🧹 Email cleanup

**What it does**: once a day, it archives inbox mail that has gone stale — mail carrying a low-priority label (like *Newsletter* or *Skip*) that has sat unread past a grace period you choose (4 days works well). Your inbox stays breathable without you ever bulk-selecting anything.

**What it never does**: delete. Archiving only removes mail from the inbox — it stays in Gmail's "All Mail", fully searchable, forever. Nothing else is touched: no sending, no spam-marking, and it only ever archives mail with the labels you designated.

**Needs**: Gmail connected. Pairs naturally with **Email triage** (which applies the labels this routine acts on), but works with any labels you already use.

**Runs**: daily (you pick the time).

**Where you see it**: a calmer inbox, and a one-line note per run in your kit's history.

**Good first routine?** Best as a companion to Email triage once its labels feel right.
