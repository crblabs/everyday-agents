# 📥 Email triage

**What it does**: once a day, it looks at inbox email that hasn't been sorted yet and applies one of a few simple labels you choose at setup — things like *Work*, *Personal*, *Needs reply*, *Newsletter*, *Skip*. Your inbox stops being one undifferentiated pile.

**What it never does**: send, reply, forward, delete, or mark as spam. It only applies labels. Anything it isn't sure about, it leaves untouched and mentions in its notes.

**Needs**: Gmail connected.

**Runs**: daily, early morning (you pick the time).

**Where you see it**: your mail is simply sorted when you open it. Each run leaves a one-line note in your kit's history ("sorted 14 emails"); the weekly review sums these up.

**Good first routine?** Yes — it's the most popular starting point, and its effect is visible the first morning.
