# ✉️ Reply radar

**What it does**: every morning, it reads your recent inbox and answers one question — *which emails actually need an answer from you?* — then puts that short list where you'll see it: a small event on today's calendar. Real questions from real people float up; noise doesn't.

**What it never does**: reply, draft into your mailbox, send, or delete. It reads and points. You answer (or don't) on your own terms.

**Needs**: Gmail and Google Calendar connected.

**Runs**: daily, early morning (you pick the time).

**Where you see it**: a calendar event at the top of your morning. Its title is the verdict — "✉️ 3 need a reply" or "✉️ nothing needs you" — and its description lists who's waiting, for what, and how long they've waited.

**Good first routine?** Yes — especially if email guilt is your particular monster.
