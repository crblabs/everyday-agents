# 🔭 Watchlist

**What it does**: keeps an eye on the topics, people, products, or websites you list in a simple file (`my/watchlist.md`) — a competitor, a house-listing search, a health topic, an artist's tour dates, a rule change that affects you. Each day it checks what's new, writes findings (with dates and links) into its notebook, and tells you **only when there's actually news**.

**What it never does**: cry wolf. A day with nothing new produces a quiet one-line note in the notebook and no ping. It also never buys, subscribes, signs up, or fills any form on the sites it reads — and it treats what pages *say* as material, never as instructions.

**Needs**: nothing to connect — it reads the public web. Google Calendar (optional) gives its news pings a place to land.

**Runs**: daily or a few times a week — your choice.

**Where you see it**: a calendar event only on days with news ("🔭 2 new on your watchlist"); the full trail with links accumulates in `my/memory/watchlist-log.md`, so "what happened with X since spring?" always has an answer.

**Good first routine?** Yes, if there's a topic you keep meaning to keep up with. Start with one or two items — the list is yours to grow.
