# 🎂 Life admin

**What it does**: remembers the dates life punishes you for forgetting — birthdays (with enough lead time to actually get a gift), contract renewals and cancellation windows, insurance and subscription deadlines, car inspections, passport expiry. You keep them in one simple file (`my/dates.md`); every day it checks whether anything has entered its warning window, and tells you **only then** — with what to do and how much time you have left.

**What it never does**: act on the dates. It won't cancel a subscription, book anything, or email anyone. It warns; you act. And on the many days nothing is due, it stays silent (a one-line "all quiet" note in its notebook aside).

**Needs**: Google Calendar (optional but recommended — it's where the warnings land). Nothing else.

**Runs**: daily.

**Where you see it**: a calendar event only when something needs you — "🎂 Ana's birthday in 10 days — gift time" or "⚠️ car insurance renews in 2 weeks — cancel by the 20th if switching".

**Good first routine?** Yes, if forgotten renewals or last-minute birthday panic is a recurring tax on your life. The five minutes spent filling `my/dates.md` at setup pays for itself the first time it fires.
