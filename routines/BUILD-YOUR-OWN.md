# Build your own

The eight routines in this catalog are **starting points, not the menu**. The kit's real muscle is the builder: describe what you wish happened automatically — in your own words, however vague — and Claude walks you through the whole thing: shaping the idea, connecting any account it needs, rehearsing it live once so you see exactly what it will do, and scheduling it. Just say:

> **"Build me a routine that…"**

## What makes a good routine

The builder will ask you these; thinking about them first is optional but helps:

1. **The job** — what should it do, concretely, in one run?
2. **The material** — which accounts or websites hold what it needs to look at?
3. **The rhythm** — daily, weekly, a few times a day? (Once an hour is the fastest possible.)
4. **The report** — how do you want to hear about it, and should a quiet day ping you at all?
5. **The nevers** — anything it must not touch?

The best routines share a shape: they **watch something you'd otherwise have to remember to check, judge it against your taste, and only interrupt you when it matters**. Sorting, summarizing, warning, preparing: great. Anything that would *act in your name* — send, delete, spend — is off the table by design; helpers prepare and flag, you act.

## Ideas people actually build

- **School mail digest** — pull the actionable bits (dates, forms, money) out of the flood of school/club emails, weekly.
- **Travel prep** — a few days before any trip on your calendar: weather there, check-in reminders, a packing nudge tuned to the destination.
- **Price watch** — the specific thing you're saving for; ping only when it actually drops.
- **Subscription audit** — a monthly sweep of your inbox for recurring charges, with a "still using this?" list.
- **Team weather report** — for a manager: what your people flagged this week across email you're cc'd on, before your 1:1s.
- **Reading queue** — collect the articles you emailed yourself all week into one Sunday digest with two-line summaries.
- **Training recap** — your week of workouts against your goal, with an honest verdict (needs the Strava connection).
- **Rent & admin paper trail** — file the recurring documents that arrive by email into a tidy log you can find at tax time.
- **Local radar** — events, roadworks, or council decisions in your town, weekly, only when there's something.
- **Language nudge** — a daily micro-lesson built from words you actually encountered (in your watchlist findings, your inbox…).

Steal any of these, or bring your own. Nothing coming to mind? That's the normal case, not the exception — say **"I don't know what I need"** and Claude mirrors your actual weeks back to you until something stings (or browse [the moments](MOMENTS.md)). And before you start, it's worth asking **"what are people wishing for?"**: the community keeps an open wishlist, and building someone's wish (then saying "share this routine") is how the catalog grows. If your idea needs an account the kit can't reach yet, the builder will say so honestly and offer the nearest workable version — see [Connections, in plain English](../docs/connections.md).

The traffic runs both ways: a routine you're proud of can go to the [community shelf](community/README.md) — scrubbed of everything personal, credited to you, human-reviewed before anyone installs it. And a wish you can't build yourself ("I wish a helper could…") is a real contribution too.
