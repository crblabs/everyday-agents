# Do you know this moment?

Nobody can list "the tasks I should automate" — that's normal, and it's not how this kit starts. Repetitive work goes invisible; you stop noticing what you always do. But **moments** are recognizable. Read these; the ones that sting are your answer.

| The moment | The answer |
|---|---|
| **Sunday evening, and the week ahead is a fog you'd rather not look at** | [🗓 Week ahead](week-ahead/ABOUT.md) |
| **That email you've owed someone for four days. You think about it more than it would take to answer it** | [✉️ Reply radar](reply-radar/ABOUT.md) |
| **"Wait — their birthday is TOMORROW?"** | [🎂 Life admin](life-admin/ABOUT.md) |
| **The renewal you meant to cancel, discovered on the bank statement** | [🎂 Life admin](life-admin/ABOUT.md) |
| **The tab you keep reopening to check if something changed** | [🔭 Watchlist](watchlist/ABOUT.md) |
| **Opening your inbox and closing it again without touching anything** | [📥 Email triage](email-triage/ABOUT.md) + [🧹 Cleanup](email-cleanup/ABOUT.md) |
| **The morning scramble: phone in one hand, coffee in the other, "what do I have today?"** | [☕ Morning brief](morning-brief/ABOUT.md) |
| **Friday: "what did I even do this week?"** | [🪞 Weekly review](weekly-review/ABOUT.md) |
| **The school/club emails you scroll past — until the one with the deadline you missed** | Build it: a school-mail digest ("build me a routine") |
| **Receipts and documents you forward to yourself "to deal with later"** | Build it: a paper trail ("build me a routine") |
| **Three emails to agree on one meeting time** | Honest answer: not automatable by this kit yet — [wish for it](../docs/community.md) |
| **"I know something's eating my time, I just couldn't tell you what"** | Say **"I don't know what I need"** — Claude looks at your actual weeks and shows you |

Two things this page is built on:

- **You don't have to generate ideas — only recognize yourself.** If nothing here stung, say "I don't know what I need" and Claude will mirror your own email and calendar back to you (with your permission, read-only): the patterns it finds are your moments, with numbers attached.
- **A moment that isn't automatable is still worth naming.** Some of your stings will map to a routine, some to something worth building, and some — honestly — to a wish for later. Claude will tell you which is which, including the "not worth it yet" ones and why.
