# 🧰 Kit news

**What it does**: once a week, it checks the kit's public home page for news — new community routines on the shelf, new packs, kit improvements — and tells you **only when something actually landed**: "2 new helpers appeared in the kit this week." Your copy was made from a snapshot; this is the helper that keeps you connected to the living kit without you ever visiting GitHub.

**What it never does**: install anything. It points; you decide — installing is always a conversation ("what's new?" to Claude). It reads only the kit's own public pages, nothing else, and a week with no news stays silent.

**Needs**: nothing to connect — the kit's home is public. Google Calendar (optional) gives its pings a place to land.

**Runs**: weekly.

**Where you see it**: a calendar event only on weeks with news — "🧰 2 new helpers in the kit — say 'what's new' to see them."

**Good first routine?** A nice fourth — costs nothing, connects you to what everyone else is building.
