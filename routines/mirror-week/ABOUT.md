# 🪩 Mirror week

**What it does**: watches your week the way a kind consultant would — quietly. For seven days it takes a daily, read-only look at the shape of your email and calendar (who wrote, what repeated, what waited, how the day was arranged) and writes its observations into its notebook. On day seven, it hands you the mirror: your week in plain words, the recurring gestures you never notice yourself making, and the two or three that a helper could genuinely take off your hands.

**What it never does**: ping you mid-week (its silence is the point), act on anything it sees, read for content rather than pattern, or continue past its week — a mirror that lingers becomes surveillance, so on day seven it tells you its job is done and asks to be stopped.

**Needs**: Gmail and/or Google Calendar connected (whichever you're willing to have mirrored). Google Calendar also gives its one final report a place to land.

**Runs**: daily for one week. Then it's over — by design.

**Where you see it**: nothing for six days; one calendar event on day seven — "🪩 your week, mirrored" — with the full report.

**Good first routine?** It's the perfect *zeroth* routine for one kind of person: the one who says "I know something's eating my time, I just couldn't tell you what" — and who'd rather have a week watched than answer questions. (In a hurry? Saying **"I don't know what I need"** gets you the same mirror from your *past* weeks, today.)
