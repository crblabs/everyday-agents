# 📣 Go-to-market pack

For early GTM — when every mention matters, the pipeline lives in three tabs, and content dies in the "I should write about that" folder.

**How to read the marks**: ✅ install today · 🔨 build it with Claude ("build me the …") · 🌠 a wish for now.

| Routine | Why it's in the pack | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentions radar** — your product's name on HN, Reddit, and the open web; pinged only on genuine mentions | The thread about you that you find three days late is a conversation you weren't in | 🔨 |
| **Pipeline digest** — deals moving vs. stalling, weekly, from your CRM | A HubSpot connection exists, so this is a real build today: the Monday answer to "where's the pipeline?" | 🔨 |
| **Launch radar** — Product Hunt / HN launches in your category | Every launch near you is either a threat, a pattern, or a partner | 🔨 |
| **Changelog-to-content draft** — the week's shipped work turned into a *draft* post, flagged for you, never published | The kit's prepare-and-flag rule at its best: assembly delegated, voice and send kept by you | 🔨 |
| **Social radar** — mentions and conversations on X/LinkedIn | 🌠 Both live behind logins no connection reaches — [wished](https://github.com/crblabs/everyday-agents/issues/6) | 🌠 |
