# 🎓 Student pack

For deadlines that arrive in swarms, readings that pile up, and mornings that need one calm voice.

| Routine | Why it's in the pack |
|---|---|
| [🎂 Life admin](../routines/life-admin/ABOUT.md) | Assignment due dates, registration windows, scholarship deadlines — each warned with enough lead time to actually do the thing |
| [☕ Morning brief](../routines/morning-brief/ABOUT.md) | Classes, what's due soon, and the one thing your first free hour should go to |
| [🔭 Watchlist](../routines/watchlist/ABOUT.md) | Internship postings, your field's news, that professor's page that updates without warning |
| [🪞 Weekly review](../routines/weekly-review/ABOUT.md) | The Sunday answer to "what did I actually get done?" — and what next week will punish you for skipping |

Suggested tweak at setup: point life-admin's warning lead times at *your* procrastination honestly — 10 days for essays beats 3.
