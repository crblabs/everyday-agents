# 🚀 Founder pack

For the person running product, go-to-market, and admin alone — where the inbox is the pipeline, the calendar is the roadmap, and nothing follows up on itself.

**How to read the marks**: ✅ install today ("add the …") · 🔨 build it with Claude in ~20 minutes ("build me the …") · 🌠 not honestly buildable yet — it's a wish, and [wishing is a contribution](../docs/community.md).

| Routine | Why it's in the pack | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [✉️ Reply radar](../routines/reply-radar/ABOUT.md), tuned *investors & leads float first* | The thread that costs you money is the one buried under forty robots | ✅ |
| **Follow-up radar** — the inverse: *your* sent mail that got no answer in N days | Silence from a lead is a task nobody's inbox shows; this is the anti-silence machine, pointed outward | 🔨 |
| **Competitor radar** — their changelogs, pricing pages, launches; weekly *what changed and what it might mean* | You check their site in stolen moments; a helper checks it every day without the doomscroll | 🔨 |
| **Investor-update draft** — monthly: what shipped, milestones, the numbers you track → a *draft* in your notes, never sent | The update you dread writing is mostly assembly; keep the judgment, delegate the assembly | 🔨 |
| [☕ Morning brief](../routines/morning-brief/ABOUT.md) | The day on one screen before the day starts | ✅ |
| **Team pulse** — what your team surfaced in Slack/Discord overnight, distilled | 🌠 No Slack/Discord connection exists yet — [wished](https://github.com/crblabs/everyday-agents/issues/4) | 🌠 |

**Honest notes for founders**: your work email is likely Google Workspace — whether Claude can connect to it is your admin's call ([the check](../docs/faq.md#is-this-for-me)). And doing serious product work? The [power-ups shelf](../docs/power-ups.md) — Pawel Huryn's PM Skills and Garry Tan's gstack — deepens what the builds above can do.
