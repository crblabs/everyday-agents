# 💼 Freelancer pack

For people whose inbox is their business: the leads, the invoices, the clients, and the silence that costs money all live in the same place.

| Routine | Why it's in the pack |
|---|---|
| [📥 Email triage](../routines/email-triage/ABOUT.md) | Clients, prospects, admin, and noise stop sharing one pile — suggested labels: *Clients*, *Leads*, *Money*, *Later* |
| [✉️ Reply radar](../routines/reply-radar/ABOUT.md) | The lead that's been waiting three days is the one that goes elsewhere; this is the anti-silence machine |
| [🗓 Week ahead](../routines/week-ahead/ABOUT.md) | Deadlines and client calls seen on Sunday instead of felt on Tuesday |
| [🔭 Watchlist](../routines/watchlist/ABOUT.md) | Your niche, your competitors, the platforms that send you work — watched so you don't have to doomscroll for it |

Natural next build: an invoice-chaser's *radar* ("who hasn't paid, how late") — a favorite thing to create with "build me a routine" once the pack has settled in.
