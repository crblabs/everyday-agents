# 🤖 AI-lab pack

For teams building *on* AI — where the ground moves weekly, a pricing change upstream rewrites your margins, and "did you see the new model?" is a daily tax.

**How to read the marks**: ✅ install today · 🔨 build it with Claude ("build me the …") · 🌠 a wish for now.

| Routine | Why it's in the pack | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Model & pricing watch** — releases, API price changes, deprecations across the providers you build on | The upstream change you learn about late is the one that costs you; this is a [watchlist](../routines/watchlist/ABOUT.md) with its eyes on Anthropic/OpenAI/Google/Mistral | 🔨 |
| **Papers digest** — arXiv on *your* niche: three papers max, each with one "why it matters to us" line | The reading pile only ever grows; a curated three beats an aspirational thirty | 🔨 |
| **Leaderboard watch** — movement on the evals your customers actually quote | You'll be asked about it in a sales call before you've seen it | 🔨 |
| **Launch radar** — AI tooling launches on HN/Product Hunt in your category | Competitors announce themselves; someone should be listening | 🔨 |
| **Token-spend sentinel** — your API bills watched for anomalies, pinged only on a spike | 🌠 No billing connection exists (provider dashboards are login-walled) — [wished](https://github.com/crblabs/everyday-agents/issues/5) | 🌠 |

**One design note baked into every build above**: these are *cry-wolf-hardened* by the kit's contract — quiet weeks stay silent, and every finding carries its link and date. In a hype-dense field, a watcher you can trust to shut up is the whole value.
