# Packs

A pack is a curated bundle of routines tuned for a season of life — someone walked this road, picked what actually helped, and wrote down why. Installing a pack is just installing its routines together, each still personalized to you during setup.

| Pack | For |
|---|---|
| [🍼 New-parent pack](new-parent.md) | The season when nothing fits in your head anymore |
| [💼 Freelancer pack](freelancer.md) | People whose inbox is their business |
| [🎓 Student pack](student.md) | Deadlines, reading, and one calm morning voice |
| [🚀 Founder pack](founder.md) | Running product, go-to-market, and admin alone |
| [🧑‍💻 Builder pack](builder.md) | Whoever owns the repo and drowns in its notifications |
| [🤖 AI-lab pack](ai-lab.md) | Teams building on AI, where the ground moves weekly |
| [📣 Go-to-market pack](go-to-market.md) | Early GTM: mentions, pipeline, launches, content |

The startup packs (last four) are honest about maturity: some routines install today (✅), most are **built with you** in about 20 minutes (🔨 — say "build me the …"), and a few are **wishes** (🌠) waiting on connections that don't exist yet — each one filed openly in [the tracker](https://github.com/crblabs/everyday-agents/issues).

Say a pack's name to Claude ("set me up with the freelancer pack") — or describe your life and let setup suggest one. **Curating a pack is a contribution too**: if you'd bundle things differently for a life you know well (caregiver, retiree, new manager…), say "I wish there were a … pack" and describe it.

Doing product or engineering work seriously? The kit also keeps a short, curated shelf of [**power-ups**](../docs/power-ups.md) — admired external skill collections (Pawel Huryn's PM Skills, Garry Tan's gstack, obra's Superpowers) that can deepen what your helpers and conversations can do. Nothing installs by default; the shelf explains who each is for.
