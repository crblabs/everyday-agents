# 🍼 New-parent pack

For the season when the days are long, the nights are broken, and nothing fits in your head anymore. This pack's job is to *be* the head that remembers.

| Routine | Why it's in the pack |
|---|---|
| [🎂 Life admin](../routines/life-admin/ABOUT.md) | Vaccination windows, daycare paperwork deadlines, the in-laws' birthdays — warned in time, remembered by nobody's tired brain |
| [🧹 Email cleanup](../routines/email-cleanup/ABOUT.md) | The inbox you haven't opened in four days stops being a source of dread |
| [☕ Morning brief](../routines/morning-brief/ABOUT.md) | One phone screen at 6am with what today actually requires — readable one-handed |
| [🪞 Weekly review](../routines/weekly-review/ABOUT.md) | Proof that things were handled this week, even when you can't remember the week |

Deliberately **not** included: reply radar — this is a season for answering less, not being reminded of it daily. Add it later when the fog lifts ("add the reply radar").
