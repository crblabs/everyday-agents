# Remember — the door into the notebook

The owner is handing you something to keep. Your job: put it where it will actually *work*, tag where it came from, and confirm in words that show them what keeping it means. Read `CLAUDE.md`'s rules first — and its **template guard**: if this is the public template rather than someone's copy, don't write; help them get their own copy first.

## Route by shape, not by topic

The whole trick of this skill is one question: **should this fact ever *fire*, or just be *findable*?**

| The memory is… | It goes to | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Date-shaped** — a birthday, renewal, deadline, expiry, "in March we need to…" | `my/dates.md` (life-admin's file) | The life-admin helper will *warn in time*. Confirm with the payoff: "noted — I'll warn you 30 days before." |
| **Watch-shaped** — "keep an eye on…", an interest, a thing that changes | `my/watchlist.md` | The watchlist helper will *check and ping on news*. |
| **About the owner** — a preference, a standing fact of their life that helpers should honor | `my/profile.md` | Every routine reads profile first; this changes behavior. |
| **Everything else** — the boiler service, Ana's allergy, the wifi guest password, where the spare key is | `my/notes.md` | The junk drawer: findable forever, acted on never. |

When a memory is two shapes at once ("Ana is gluten-free *and* her birthday is March 14"), split it — the date fires, the fact files.

## The entry

Dated, plain, tagged with its source per the kit convention — almost always *(you told me — YYYY-MM-DD)*; if the owner is passing on someone else's claim or a guess, say so in the tag. In `notes.md`, newest first, one entry per memory. Never quote more than the owner gave you; never enrich a fact with assumptions dressed as memory.

## Confirm with meaning, then commit

The confirmation states **where it went and what will happen** — that's what teaches the owner the system: "In your dates — the life-admin helper warns you 30 days ahead" / "In your notes — it won't trigger anything, but ask me anytime and it's there." If the owner clearly expected an *action* ("remember to reply to Paul") point at the truth kindly: the kit doesn't act, but the reply radar already surfaces waiting mail — or a date entry can nag on a day they choose.

Commit (`remember YYYY-MM-DD: <one plain line, no secrets in the message>`), push. Push failure: keep the commit, say so plainly.

## The retrieve side

"What do you know about X?" needs no ceremony: read `my/` (notes, profile, dates, watchlist), answer with each fact's source tag out loud ("you told me in June…"). Finding nothing is an honest answer — better than a guess wearing memory's clothes. To forget something: delete the entry on the owner's word, commit; deletion is the owner's absolute right over their own notebook.
